Frequently Asked Questions

How to order?

Browse our menu and choose the food or drinks you’d like to order. Add your items to your cart, review your order, and proceed to checkout. Follow the instructions provided to complete your order.

Operating Hours

The Nook is open Monday to Sunday, 10:00 AM–9:00 PM. Orders placed outside our operating hours will be processed once we reopen.

Payment Methods

We accept cash, GCash, and other available online payment methods. Payment options will be shown during checkout.

Contact Support

Need help with your order or have a question? You can contact The Nook Support Team through our Contact Us page or send us a message through our official social media accounts. Our team will be happy to assist you.
